## English draft

Plan a local journey in Christchurch

Use Metro’s official information to prepare a local trip during your first week.

Choose a destination and check Metro’s Getting started page for its journey-planning tools and network information. Keep your accommodation address and travel details private; Sajilo NZ does not collect them.

Before travelling, use the official site to check the current route, timetable, fares and payment options. This guide does not quote a fare or promise that a service will run at a particular time. Official tools require internet.

Check the accessible-travel and help links on Metro’s page if you need further information. Tick the local-journey planning task when you have made your plan; the tick does not confirm service availability.
