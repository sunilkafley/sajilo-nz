# Explore city discovery

Acceptance:
- Explore has no repeated Get to know Aotearoa card grid. Learn about New Zealand opens Home's existing section, scrolls it into view and focuses its heading, preserving language.
- Find information about your city keeps all five existing city destinations, each with a real representative landmark photograph and descriptive alternative text.
- City imagery is bundled locally, has visible expandable attribution with author/source/license links, and uses responsive cards without horizontal overflow.
- Photos do not assert current services, availability or content verification. Existing publication gates remain unchanged.

Photo files, original Commons URLs, authors and licenses are recorded in city-photo-sources.json. Photos are displayed with CSS cropping; no original source pixels were edited. Render automatic deployment remains unchanged.
