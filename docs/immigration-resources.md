# Immigration resources acceptance criteria

- The immigration hub provides official INZ links for student, work, visitor and residence information, including both requested student links, and links to the INZ visa finder and Immigration Advisers Authority.
- The news section links to the current INZ news centre and does not present a static announcement as live news. It requires an internet connection.
- These are English navigation links, not published immigration advice or translated articles. Published guidance and translations continue to require the existing human review process. No verification date is asserted.
- The resources remain visible independently of the article search and article service availability. No eligibility assessment is provided.
